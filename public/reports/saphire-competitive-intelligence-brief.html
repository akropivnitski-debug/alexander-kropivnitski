Saphire Saffron Google Ads Account

trysaphire.com (Happy Chews)

01

Executive Summary

Saphire competes on two fronts, and the account's own Auction Insights exports make both visible without a single external search. On the brand/legitimate front, direct saffron-gummy peers (JoySpring) and larger kids-wellness brands (Hiya, OLLY, First Day, EllaOla, BrainMD) are contesting parent attention. On the auction front (the more urgent, fixable one), Amazon resellers and affiliate content farms are intercepting Saphire's own branded demand, and the Shopping "Brand" campaign is bleeding impression share into the entire kids-supplement category and even raw culinary-saffron sellers.

Biggest threat
62%

Peak query overlap from amazon.com against Saphire's own Brand Search campaign, with a 90%+ top-of-page rate. Brand-intent clicks are being won by resellers, not trysaphire.com.

Biggest opportunity
90+

Distinct advertiser domains surfacing on the Shopping "Brand" campaign: proof the campaign isn't isolating brand queries. Segmenting it is a high-confidence, fast win.

03

Competitor profiles

JoySpring (Mood Munchies)

Account dataMarket research

Overlap: Near-identical actives (saffron + GABA + passionflower + St. John's Wort). Surfaces directly in Shopping brand auction insights (~11% IS).

Model: Multi-retailer presence (Amazon, Walmart, niche wellness stores), a listing brand rather than a funnel brand.

Proof: 4.2/5, under 100 reviews on most listings, vs. Saphire's claimed 4.85/5 and 2,000+ reviews. Saphire's social-proof asset is stronger but under-featured in paid creative.

Gap: No visible quiz funnel, PDP remarketing, or angle-testing. Saphire's 15+ live creative batches (ADHD, Natural Alternative, Bed Wetting, Quiz Funnel) is a structural edge most auditors would assume doesn't exist in this category.

Hiya Health & OLLY (mass/VC-backed)

Account data

Threat type: Category-education capture, not product-level. Hiya owns "kids health vitamin" as a DTC category name; OLLY owns it on-shelf at Target/CVS/Walgreens. Neither claims saffron specificity or the Ritalin-comparison clinical angle, a wedge Saphire can own outright.

BrainMD (Dr. Amen)

Account data

Threat type: Authority-register overlap: parents researching "cognitive support for kids" land here on credentials, not gummies. Saphire's 2019 clinical trial citation is the direct counter.

Affiliate / "best of" content cluster

Account data

10best-choices.com, topcertifiedsupplements.com, toproductsreviews.com, thetop5tested.com, our-top5.com, supplement-review.co, momjunction.com, thefullest.com. Not competitors on formula, but arbitrage on the comparison-stage query ("best saffron gummies for kids"), typically monetized via Amazon affiliate links. They are winning the exact moment Saphire's clinical-trial and review-volume story would win, because Saphire has no owned page built for that query.

05

Threats vs. opportunities

Threat

Brand-term spend may be subsidizing Amazon's checkout economics instead of Saphire's own subscription LTV.

Threat

"Brand" Shopping campaign has ~90 overlapping advertisers. In practice, it's a mislabeled non-brand campaign.

Opportunity

No competitor owns the Ritalin-comparison clinical angle. Saphire's strongest asset is under-deployed against JoySpring specifically.

Opportunity

Affiliate "best of" sites are farming a query Saphire could own with one comparison landing page + matching NB ad group.

06

Recommended actions

Quick wins (this week)

  1. Placement/domain-exclude confirmed affiliate farms (10best-choices.com + peers) on PMax/Display where eligible.
  2. Segment the Shopping "Brand" campaign's listing groups to exclude raw-spice and non-supplement matches.
  3. Review Bed Wetting PMax asset group for audience/query bleed into diaper-brand adjacent traffic.

Strategic (this quarter)

  1. Open the Amazon-reseller conversation: MAP enforcement or a deliberate "buy direct" incentive in brand ad copy.
  2. Build a "JoySpring alternative" / "best saffron gummies" comparison page and matching NB ad group to reclaim affiliate-owned queries.
  3. Stand up a monthly Auction Insights review as a standing account-health check, not a one-time audit line item.
Source: account Auction Insights exports (Search & Shopping, Brand campaigns) + public site/marketplace research. Confidence: high on auction data, moderate on qualitative competitor positioning.
